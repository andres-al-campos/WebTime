Where I'd differ from the spec.
  • You asked for today's session card to look like the past-day cards. I kept them different: the live card is an interactive ledger (percent left, time-left + 10% bonus + rollover, End button); a past-day card is a finished summary. Matching them would either strip today's controls or pad the past-day cards with a bar and a tag they don't need. They share the card shell, eyebrow type, and 12px Mono figures, so they read as one family without pretending to be one thing.
  • The vertical-space constraint I did honor literally: 508px budget, today's stack fits inside it with room, and overflow stays hidden on today so a scrollbar can't appear.
  • I dropped the Session-rules card from past days. Stored settings are today's rules; showing them over a past day would assert they were the rules then, which the data can't support. The per-session x/y and cooldown figures already say what the rules were.
