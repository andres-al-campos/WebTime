Why here and not the topbar.
  • The topbar has no free corner — nav, date, rule, domain and gear already fill it, and setTopbarDate() already marks a past day by dropping the “· Today” suffix.
  • The usage card is already the “this site, this day” total (#detail-usage-card), so showing a past day there is the same card with different numbers rather than new furniture.
  • Scrolling with the content means no height is permanently spent on a header that only matters when you first land.
